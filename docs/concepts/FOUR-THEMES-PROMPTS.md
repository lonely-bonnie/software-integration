## icon

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original Windows application icon for Rider Console, independent of four switchable visual themes.
Primary request: design a striking, sharply angular, premium sci-fi software emblem. A single bold abstract R-like command glyph built from three interlocking beveled metallic blade segments and a central luminous diagonal gap, suggesting an integrated control core or portal. Clean silhouette readable at 32px and 16px. No helmet, no character face, no game logo.
Composition: centered square icon, symbol fills about 78% of canvas with comfortable padding. Transparent background outside the emblem. Thick shapes, few parts, balanced negative space.
Style: polished dimensional application badge, cool gunmetal and satin platinum faces, controlled electric cyan lighting along one edge, a small coral red accent on one separate segment. Crisp precision edges and subtle realistic material depth, not a flat generic letter.
Constraints: actual transparent alpha background, no enclosing rounded-square plate, no texture background, no drop shadow outside the mark, no tiny screws, no complex filigree, no extra symbols, no text, no watermark. One unified recognisable app mark.

## abyss

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: cinematic ultra-wide hero artwork for a Windows dashboard's Subnautica-inspired deep-ocean theme.
Scene: an alien deep-sea bioluminescent reef at twilight, immersive translucent teal water, distant towering rock arches, shafts of blue sunlight from high above, a white and safety-orange compact exploration submersible with a dark panoramic cockpit and bright cyan headlamps on the right foreground. Strange graceful translucent jellyfish and glowing turquoise and violet coral near the craft, a tiny faraway creature silhouette for scale. Underwater wonder and quiet tension, believable caustics and floating particles.
Composition: approximately 2.4:1 very wide landscape. Put the submersible and luminous reef entirely on the RIGHT 48%, keep the LEFT 52% as calm dark midnight blue water and faint atmospheric haze with low detail and no bright objects, reserved for real dashboard text. All important craft features inside frame.
Style: high-end cinematic game concept art, richly detailed but clear, white-orange industrial exploration hardware, luminous marine ecology. Atmospheric blue and turquoise with small warm orange accents.
Constraints: generate original concept art inspired by Subnautica's underwater exploration aesthetic; no game logos, no interface, no HUD, no gauges, no text, no watermarks, no border. Do not put a helmet or robot as the focal subject.

## anime

Asset type: ultra-wide hero illustration for a Windows dashboard's premium anime-game theme.
Primary request: a polished original adult anime sci-fi navigator, a young adult woman with icy silver hair and subtle violet highlights, expressive cool violet eyes, a sleek dark navy and white futuristic jacket with restrained cyan circuitry and a flowing violet scarf, tasteful layered technical clothing, composed confident expression. Upper-torso portrait on the RIGHT, one slender holographic star-navigation instrument near her hand, and a glowing star map behind her right shoulder. Precise attractive character design, mature proportions.

Style: premium 2D anime / gacha-game key visual, beautifully drawn expressive face, crisp clean line art, refined cel shading, painterly soft light, luminous violet / ice-blue / pink accents. Looks hand illustrated rather than photorealistic or a 3D doll.
Constraints: original character, no specific existing game character, no logo, no text, no UI, no HUD, no watermark, no borders, no childish proportions, no exaggerated anatomy, no revealing costume. Focus on an elegant futuristic navigator and dramatic mood.
